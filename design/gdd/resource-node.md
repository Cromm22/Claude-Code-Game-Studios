## Overview

Resource Node is Terranova's gathering system: the scattered, harvestable points across the hand-built map that are the squad's only source of raw materials — and the primary faucet of ecological disturbance. Nodes come in three tiers, each yielding one material and emitting a tier-scaled `Gather` disturbance when harvested: **Light nodes** yield Biomass Shard (quietest), **Medium nodes** yield Mineral Core, and **Heavy nodes** yield Resonant Ore (loudest). A player gathers by approaching a node within range and committing a tap-hold; on completion the server validates the action, fires a `Gather` emission into the Ecological Disturbance field at the node's position, grants the material to the gatherer's personal inventory, and starts the node's depletion-and-respawn cycle. Every gather is therefore a deliberate trade: the materials fund survival and the escape Beacon, but the louder the node, the more legible the squad becomes to the predator. Resource Node exists to put that trade into the loop as a per-gather **approach decision** (the RN-owned Beat-1 cost-read: every node advertises its tier cost before you commit), with the dramatic loud-vs-quiet payoff peaking at the once-per-run RESONANT Beacon push — not a continuously agonized per-second choice (the moment-to-moment *consequence* that would make every gather a weighing is Beat-2, ED + Predator-AI-owned and forward-pending; see Player Fantasy). Without RN the squad has nothing to craft and nothing to weigh, and "Quiet Is Power" has no faucet to act on. It is server-authoritative end to end (gather requests are validated against server-tracked proximity, never trusted from the client) and owns node placement, per-tier yield rates, and respawn cadence, while deferring material definitions to Crafting & Items, disturbance magnitudes and decay to Ecological Disturbance, and the input gesture to the Player Controller.

## Player Fantasy

You are not a harvester. You are a creature deciding how much noise its survival is worth.

Most of the run you move quiet — a hand on a Biomass node, a soft take, the world none the wiser, and the small clean relief of having gotten away with it. Then the Beacon needs Resonant Ore, and there is only the loud node, and you crouch beside it while the squad scatters to cover, and you hold the gather, and you hold your breath, and you wait to find out what you just told the dark. Gathering in Terranova is the rhythm of those two breaths: the quiet ones you take freely, and the loud one you pay for. The loud moment RN delivers is the Beacon's RESONANT demand: you play quiet until the Beacon's recipe forces the push. RN does **not** claim an ongoing chosen-restraint-reward loop — that would require a pre-Beacon RESONANT consumer, which is out of scope.

The tap-hold is a promise you make with your whole hand — *I am choosing to be heard.* Your power here is never measured in what you gathered; it is measured in how little of yourself you had to give away to the things that are listening. So you ration it: you take the quiet nodes when you can and the loud ones only when the Beacon leaves you no choice. *(Honesty note (RN-2): RN delivers this rationing as a moment-to-moment **choice** — the per-tier cost is legible at the decision point, Beat 1. Whether that choice compounds into a run-by-run "expert at being small" mastery loop depends on the world actually answering the noise, which is ED + Predator-AI-owned and unauthored, Beat 2 / H.29b / OQ.1. RN does not claim the mastery payoff it cannot yet deliver.)*

**Beat 1 — RN-OWNED, DELIVERABLE NOW.** Before a player commits, RN advertises the cost. Every tier has a readable silhouette at distance, a tier-keyed idle audio signal, and an extraction duration the player learns through play. A Heavy (RESONANT) column is visible and audible from across the room — crossing toward it is already a visible, legible choice to be heard. This "advertise before you touch it" approach-decision is the restraint signal RN delivers without ED or Predator AI: the player reads tier cost, weighs exposure, and chooses. That IS Pillar 1 (Quiet Is Power), grounded entirely in RN's owned surface.

**Beat 2 — CONTINGENT on unauthored ED + Predator AI.** The world's *response* to a gather — flora saturation, predator steering, the field tell that confirms the dark noticed — is forward-pending on Ecological Disturbance and Predator AI (H.29b, OQ.1). RN does not own or promise that consequence; it is a hard fantasy dependency logged here and down-scoped honestly.

> **Downstream obligation (RN-2, logged at OQ.1, tested by H.29b):** the restraint *payoff* — a perceptible, tier-scaled "the world heard it" consequence read at the decision point — is **ED + Predator-AI-owned and currently unauthored**. ## Detailed Design

### Core Rules

**CR.1 — Node anatomy.** Every Resource Node is a hand-placed map instance with a server-side record (`NodeRecord`) holding: `nodeId` (stable, unique), `tier` (`Light | Medium | Heavy`), `material` (`BIOMASS | MINERAL | RESONANT`), `worldPosition` (cached `Vector3`, server truth), `state`, `gathererPlayerId` (active lock or `nil`), `lockTimestamp` (server time the lock was set, drives the T5 safety-valve lock-age check, or `nil`), `respawnTimestamp`, and `generationToken`. The server enumerates all nodes at startup via `CollectionService` tag `"ResourceNode"`; nodes are server-resident regardless of client streaming (StreamingEnabled).

```luau
--!strict
type NodeState = "Available" | "Gathering" | "Depleted" | "Respawning"
type NodeRecord = {
    nodeId: string, tier: "Light"|"Medium"|"Heavy", material: "BIOMASS"|"MINERAL"|"RESONANT",
    worldPosition: Vector3, state: NodeState,
    gathererPlayerId: number?,        -- Player.UserId or nil (never the Player instance)
    lockTimestamp: number?,           -- workspace:GetServerTimeNow() at lock-set (drives T5 lock-age) or nil
    respawnTimestamp: number?,        -- ABSOLUTE server time (workspace:GetServerTimeNow()) or nil
    generationToken: number,          -- run-epoch token; see E.14
}
```

**CR.2 — Three tiers (locked tier→material→emission; RN owns yield/extraction/respawn):**

| Tier | Material | ED `Gather` magnitude | Yield/gather | Extraction | Respawn |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Light | `BIOMASS` | `MAGNITUDE_GATHER_LIGHT = 0.15` (ED-owned) | `NODE_YIELD_LIGHT = 1` | `NODE_EXTRACT_TIME_LIGHT = 3.5 s` | `NODE_RESPAWN_TIME_LIGHT = 30 s` |
| Medium | `MINERAL` | `MAGNITUDE_GATHER_MEDIUM = 0.25` (ED-owned) | `NODE_YIELD_MEDIUM = 1` | `NODE_EXTRACT_TIME_MEDIUM = 5.0 s` | `NODE_RESPAWN_TIME_MEDIUM = 60 s` |
| Heavy | `RESONANT` | `MAGNITUDE_GATHER_HEAVY = 0.40` (ED-owned) | `NODE_YIELD_HEAVY = 1` | `NODE_EXTRACT_TIME_HEAVY = 8.0 s` | `NODE_RESPAWN_TIME_HEAVY = 120 s` |

RESONANT (Heavy) is the **Beacon-gating material** (the Escape Beacon recipe requires 3 RESONANT). Its scarcity is a **risk bottleneck, not a supply bottleneck** (RN-3): with ≥4 Heavy nodes a squad never has to wait the 120 s respawn, so supply is not the constraint — what makes RESONANT *expensive* is that the loudest tier (`0.40`) carries the longest extraction (8.5 s *= the derived `gatherDuration(Heavy) = TAP_HOLD_COMMIT_DURATION + NODE_EXTRACT_TIME_HEAVY` at default `TAP_HOLD=0.5`; see D.2 — not a pinned literal, it floats with the PC-owned `TAP_HOLD` knob*), committing the player stationary and maximally legible exactly when the Beacon push has already heated the field. `NODE_RESPAWN_TIME_HEAVY` is therefore an honest *rarely-binds backstop*, not the scarcity lever (see Tuning Knobs / OQ.9).

**CR.3 — Single-charge depletion.** One completed gather depletes a node; it yields no further material until it respawns. Scarcity is expressed through respawn time and placement density, not multi-charge veins. (Multi-charge was rejected: two players on one Light node would produce 2 BIOMASS in a single extraction window, collapsing `canisterCycleTime` below the locked 9.09 s floor — see Formulas.)

**CR.4 — Two-phase gather sequence.** A gather is:
1. **Arm** — proximity is detected by a **server-side arm scan running at `ARM_SCAN_HZ = 10`** (every 0.1 s) on a `task.spawn`'d loop (`while true do task.wait(1/ARM_SCAN_HZ) … end` — never deprecated `wait()`). Each tick, for each ALIVE player, the server reads `HumanoidRootPart.Position` and tests against ALL Available nodes (plain all-pairs: at 4 players × ~dozens of nodes this is ~2,400 cheap distance² tests/s — trivial; no spatial grid required). When a player is found inside `GATHER_PROXIMITY_RADIUS = 4 studs` (PC-owned) of an `Available` node (in dark zones, only if the player's lantern is raised, per PC DC-5), the server, on the FIRST in-range tick since the last completion/death/disconnect, records `firstArmTimestamp[playerId][nodeId] = workspace:GetServerTimeNow()` AT SCAN-DETECTION TIME (not at send) and fires `GatherNodeArmed(nodeId)` to that client. **On EVERY in-range tick (including the first), it refreshes `positionAtArm[playerId][nodeId] = rootPart.Position`** — `positionAtArm` is the last confirmed in-range position, NOT frozen at first detection (decoupled from the hold anchor; see CR.5). On leaving `GATHER_PROXIMITY_RADIUS + GATHER_JITTER_TOLERANCE` (disarm radius is the larger of the two — arm at `GATHER_PROXIMITY_RADIUS`, disarm at `+ GATHER_JITTER_TOLERANCE`, so a player hovering the boundary does not flap arm/disarm: simple hysteresis) it fires `GatherNodeDisarmed(nodeId)` and **evicts `positionAtArm` + the client prompt state ONLY** — a **pre-commit** disarm does **NOT** evict the `firstArmTimestamp` hold anchor (CR.5 firstArm anchor / H.32). Pre-commit range-exit is **not** a hold-clock cancel; only the *post-commit* paths evict `firstArmTimestamp` (completion T2, post-commit range-exit T3, death/disconnect T4, safety valve T5). (Round-5 B3 reconciliation: the prior "evicts the arm record" singular was a stale sibling of the round-4 two-record decoupling — `firstArmTimestamp` FROZEN vs `positionAtArm` REFRESHED — and contradicted the H.32 anchor-persists guarantee.) Client renders the prompt/ring. The arm scan is **ADVISORY** (drives the prompt only; commit re-measures proximity authoritatively), so the 10 Hz cadence never affects gather *correctness* — only how promptly the prompt appears. Timers (extraction, respawn, safety-valve) MUST be built on a mockable scheduling interface for testability (H.6/H.19/H.20/H.21).
2. **Commit** — client completes the `TAP_HOLD_COMMIT_DURATION = 500 ms` tap-hold (PC-owned gesture) and fires `RequestGather({nodeId})` — the system's **only** client-initiated event. `ProximityPrompt` is **NOT used** — no `ProximityPrompt` Instance is placed on node Parts; the client renders its own gather-prompt widget on `GatherNodeArmed`. Per the **commit-and-wait (hands-free)** model: the 0.5 s tap-hold COMMITS the gather, and extraction then runs hands-free — the player need NOT maintain contact with the input during extraction, but must STAY IN RANGE (leaving cancels via T3).
3. **Validate & lock** — server re-validates (CR.5) and, in a **single synchronous non-yielding critical section** (see CR.6), sets `gathererPlayerId`, sets `lockTimestamp = workspace:GetServerTimeNow()`, and transitions the node to `Gathering`. A second request for a locked node is rejected with reason `node-reserved`. Clarification on `pcall`: a `pcall` wrapping a **non-yielding** call (or the whole non-yielding section) is permitted — `pcall` itself does not yield. The prohibition is specifically on any `task.wait` / awaited RemoteFunction / yielding service call between the `Available` read and the `gathererPlayerId` write.
4. **Extract** — the server runs the per-tier extraction timer (`NODE_EXTRACT_TIME_tier`). Extraction is **HANDS-FREE after commit**: the player need not hold any button during extraction; only leaving range cancels. The extraction proximity watcher **REUSES the same 10Hz arm-scan tick** (no separate per-gather coroutine): each tick, any node in `Gathering` state checks that its `gathererPlayerId` player is still within `GATHER_PROXIMITY_RADIUS + GATHER_JITTER_TOLERANCE`; if not, fire T3 within ≤100ms of detection, non-yielding.
5. **Resolve** — at extraction end the server runs, **in order and synchronously (no yield)**: **(0) RUN/STATE RE-CHECK (the literal first step, round-4 ruling):** synchronously re-read `runActive` AND `node.state`. If `runActive == false` OR `node.state ~= "Gathering"` (the run ended, or T3/T4/T5 already released this node this tick), **abort as a cancel** — no ED emit, no grant, no `OnGatherCompleted`, no state write; the releasing handler already owns the cleanup. This re-check is non-yielding, so the extraction-timer callback and a same-tick `RunEnded`/`T3` cannot interleave — whichever runs first wins and the loser no-ops on this guard. Only if the guard passes does resolve continue: (a) ED `Emit` (`pcall`-wrapped, non-yielding) → (b) fire `OnGatherCompleted(playerId, nodeId, materialType, count)` — this IS the material grant (Crafting writes on receipt; RN does NOT also call an inventory API directly; there is exactly ONE inventory write path) → (c) set `state = "Depleted"` + schedule respawn — **set Depleted BEFORE clearing the lock** → (d) clear lock (`gathererPlayerId`/`lockTimestamp`) + arm record. Steps (0)–(d) are atomic from every other handler's perspective: `state` is `"Depleted"` before the lock fields are cleared.

**CR.5 — Server authority (never trust the client).** `RequestGather` carries `{nodeId}` only — no position, material, or count. Before locking, the server validates, in this exact order: payload shape → rate limit → player alive (not dead/respawning) → node exists in registry → node `Available` → node unreserved → **arm-timestamp hold** (first a nil-guard — if `firstArmTimestamp[playerId][nodeId] == nil` reject `not-armed`, since the player never armed or the anchor was evicted [`--!strict`: the arm-record map is not a `NodeRecord` field, so a `nil − number` would otherwise error]; then `now − firstArmTimestamp[playerId][nodeId] ≥ TAP_HOLD_COMMIT_DURATION`, using `workspace:GetServerTimeNow()`) → **teleport-delta sanity** (`(currentPosition − positionAtArm).Magnitude ≤ GATHER_MAX_TELEPORT_DELTA`; reject `out-of-range` if violated — closes same-frame teleport+fire bypass for MVP) → server-measured proximity (`(rootPart.Position − node.worldPosition).Magnitude ≤ GATHER_PROXIMITY_RADIUS + GATHER_JITTER_TOLERANCE`) → inventory full check → lantern-raised (if the node sits in a dark zone). Each failing stage prevents later-stage execution (H.35). The server reads `local rootPart = character:FindFirstChild("HumanoidRootPart") :: BasePart?` with a nil guard before accessing `.Position` (`--!strict`). Rate limit: `1 / 1.5 s` sustained, burst 2 within any 3 s window (per-player token bucket, cleared on disconnect). Client-supplied hold claims and `ProximityPrompt.Triggered` are never trusted as proof the hold elapsed.

**firstArm anchor vs positionAtArm — two DECOUPLED records (B2 + round-4 ruling).** The two arm-records serve different purposes and are deliberately decoupled:
- **`firstArmTimestamp[playerId][nodeId]` (hold anchor — FROZEN at first entry).** The 500 ms hold gate is anchored to the FIRST arm-scan detection since the last completion/death/disconnect for this player×node pair. **A pre-commit walk-out-and-back does NOT reset the 500 ms clock** (CR.4 step 1 evicts only `positionAtArm`, never this anchor). The anchor persists until the gather **completes (T2)**, a **post-commit** range-exit cancels it (T3), the player **dies/disconnects (T4)**, or the **safety valve (T5)** fires — at which point `firstArmTimestamp` is evicted. **Intended consequence (round-5, documented so it is not re-flagged):** because the anchor persists, re-approaching a previously-armed node allows an immediate commit (the 500 ms hold is not re-required). This is intended and safe — it saves only the 0.5 s hold, while the per-tier extraction (3.5–8 s, in-range, hands-free) remains the real commitment, and the server-owned timestamp (H.6) still rejects any never-armed instant-fire. Freezing keeps boundary jitter from punishing a legitimate hold; it is the player-friendly anchor for the hold gate (H.32).
- **`positionAtArm[playerId][nodeId]` (teleport reference — REFRESHED every in-range tick).** This is the LAST confirmed in-range position, re-captured each 10 Hz scan tick the player is in range (CR.4 step 1) — it is NOT frozen at first detection. Freezing it would false-reject a legal walk-out-and-back (the player legitimately moves >8 studs across the arm window and returns), so it tracks the live in-range position instead.

**Teleport-delta (B5):** `GATHER_MAX_TELEPORT_DELTA = 8 studs`. At `RequestGather`, if `(currentPosition − positionAtArm).Magnitude > GATHER_MAX_TELEPORT_DELTA`, reject `out-of-range`. Because `positionAtArm` is the last in-range scan position (≤ one scan period, ~0.1 s old), this catches a same-frame teleport-then-fire (a jump of >8 studs in the last ≤0.1 s) while a legal walk-out-and-back — whose every in-range tick refreshed `positionAtArm` — passes. Closes the same-frame teleport+fire bypass for MVP. Full `:SetNetworkOwner(nil)` hardening remains OQ.10.

**CR.6 — One gatherer per node (exclusive lock + mandated non-yielding critical section).** A node is gathered by at most one player at a time. The check-and-set of the lock — `read node.state == Available` → `set gathererPlayerId` → `set state = Gathering` — **MUST execute as one non-yielding critical section**: it contains no `task.wait`, no awaited RemoteFunction, and no `pcall` wrapping a yielding call between the read and the write. Because Luau is single-threaded and cooperatively scheduled, a critical section with no yield point cannot be interleaved — two same-tick `RequestGather` calls are processed one fully before the other, so the first valid request locks and the second sees `Gathering` and is rejected. **This guarantee is void if a yield is introduced into the section**; the non-yielding constraint is therefore a hard implementation contract (tested by H.9 via two real `task.spawn` coroutines with yield-injection proving the guarantee), not an incidental property. The lock releases on success (T2), cancel (T3), death/disconnect/run-end (T4), or a safety-valve timeout (T5: lock age > `NODE_EXTRACT_TIME_tier + GATHER_LOCK_TIMEOUT_BUFFER`, `GATHER_LOCK_TIMEOUT_BUFFER = 2.0 s`). Double-grant and double-emit are therefore impossible.

**CR.7 — Node placement.** Nodes are hand-authored on the single MVP map (consistent with the "NOT procedural" anti-pillar). Placement density and node counts are a level-design obligation enforced by server-init config gates:

- **`MIN_LIGHT_NODES = 6`** (hard minimum): ensures D.2 parallelism for up to 4 players holds and `min(P, nodeCount_Light)` never hits 0 for any squad size.
- **`MIN_MEDIUM_NODES = 4`** (hard minimum): same parallelism guarantee for MINERAL.
- **Heavy ≥ 4 minimum, PREFER ≥ 5**: the exactly-4 case leaves a 2-player squad only a 1-node margin if any Heavy node is in an adverse state at Beacon-push time (see D.2). ≥5 is the preferred level-design target. Nodes must be spread to prevent single-cluster camp, so a squad can assemble the Beacon's 3 RESONANT within a 5–20 min run without farming one respawning node (`3 × 120 s` solo-camp = 6 min, exceeding the run window — exploration is forced). **Dark-zone spacing floor (PC DC-5 / OQ.13(b), added 2026-06-19):** in dark zones, adjacent gatherable nodes MUST be spaced ≥ ~10 studs apart so a single lantern-raise cannot clear a dense cluster for one Light pulse (closes the hoard-gather degenerate — the Light cost is per lantern-hold, so spacing forces re-raises / sustained hold across a cluster). Level-design placement obligation; couples to PC C.3.6 + the Predator-AI prototype's `NODE_MIN_SPACING` finding.
- **`HEAVY_NODE_MIN_RESPAWN_STAGGER = 15 s`**: no two Heavy nodes may share a respawn timer within this window (checked at server-init, H.22-class) — prevents WCAG flash-accumulation from simultaneous multi-node completion spikes during a Beacon-push.

### States and Transitions

States: **Available** (gatherable, unlocked) · **Gathering** (locked, extraction running) · **Depleted** (harvested, respawn pending) · **Respawning** (optional ~1 s reform visual; if unimplemented, Depleted → Available directly). The server broadcasts `OnNodeStateChanged(nodeId, state, respawnIn?)` to all clients; clients buffer last-known state per `nodeId` for correct stream-in recovery (RemoteEvents are not streaming-gated; Instances are).

**Join-time bootstrap:** on player join / character respawn the server fires `OnNodeFullStateSnapshot(stateTable)` giving the current state of every node; the client merges it before rendering affordances (replaces bare "buffer last-known" with an explicit bootstrap; H.33).

**`OnNodeStateChanged` payload** for the `Gathering` state includes `extractionDuration` and `extractionStartTimestamp` (= `node.lockTimestamp`, the single `workspace:GetServerTimeNow()` value captured at lock-set in CR.4 step 3 — NOT a fresh call at broadcast time; H.38 asserts this equality) so the HUD can compute progress without a drift-prone local timer. `respawnIn` (when present) is server-computed `respawnTimestamp − GetServerTimeNow()` at broadcast time.

**Same-tick race rule (mechanized, round-4):** the mechanism is the synchronous step-(0) guard in T2 resolve (CR.4 step 5), which re-reads `runActive` and `node.state == "Gathering"` with no yield before any emit/grant. Two same-tick cases:
- **T2 vs run-end (T4 / RunEnded).** If extraction-complete and run-end land on the same Heartbeat, the order in which the two callbacks run is not guaranteed, but the outcome is: if `RunEnded` ran first it set `runActive = false`, so T2's step-(0) aborts (cancel — no grant/emit); if T2's resolve ran first it completes the grant and depletes the node before `runActive` flips. The **step-(0) re-read is what makes this safe** — without it, a T2 callback that fired just before `RunEnded` on the same tick would grant into an ended run. This is the failure mode round-3 flagged: the rule is no longer merely asserted, it is the literal first line of resolve.
- **T2 vs T3 (range-exit).** Both guard on `node.state == "Gathering"`. Whichever runs first transitions the node out of `Gathering` (T3 → `Available`; T2 → `Depleted`) before yielding; the other then sees `state ~= "Gathering"` at its own guard and no-ops. So same-tick T2/T3 is **order-independent** — exactly one of {grant, cancel} occurs, never both, never neither.

See E.5.

**Cancellation needs no new client event.** Range-exit (T3), death/disconnect/run-end (T4), and the safety valve (T5) cover every abort path, so RN stays at exactly one client-initiated event (`RequestGather`). An explicit player abort is simply moving out of range during extraction.

**`ResetAllNodes()` — full specification (round-4; referenced by T6 / E.14).** Called once at run reset (run-lifecycle owned; RN subscribes). It runs **synchronously, no yield**, in this order so no in-flight callback can act on a stale node:
1. **Increment every node's `generationToken`** first — this invalidates all in-flight **respawn** `task.delay` callbacks (E.14: each callback closes over the token captured at depletion and no-ops on mismatch), before any state is touched. (Round-5 correction: the **extraction**-timer T2 callback does NOT check the generation token — it is covered separately by its own step-(0) `runActive`/`node.state` guard; do not add a spurious token check to the extraction timer.)
2. **Release every locked node** — for each node in `Gathering`, clear `gathererPlayerId` + `lockTimestamp` and evict its arm record (no emit, no grant — same as a T4 release).
3. **Set every node to `Available`** and clear `respawnTimestamp`.
4. **Broadcast** `OnNodeFullStateSnapshot(stateTable)` once (a single full-state push, not per-node `OnNodeStateChanged` spam) so every client re-bootstraps to the reset state.

Because steps 1–3 are non-yielding, no respawn or extraction callback scheduled before the reset can fire between them; step 1 (token bump) is the hard guarantee that any that fire *after* the reset no-op. **`runActive` ordering (round-5 S5-A):** the run-lifecycle owner sets `runActive = false` before (or as) it invokes `ResetAllNodes()`; but RN does NOT depend on a specific ordering — a T2 extraction callback firing at any point during or after the reset aborts on its step-(0) guard because that guard checks BOTH `runActive == false` AND `node.state ~= "Gathering"`, and step 2/3 here clear the lock and set `Available`, so *either* condition alone makes T2 no-op. Tested by H.16 (token invalidation) + H.15 (locked-node release on run reset).

### Interactions with Other Systems

- **Ecological Disturbance (RN is publisher — ED C.3.1, locked).** At T2 the server calls `DisturbanceService:Emit("Gather", node.worldPosition, <tier magnitude>, gathererPlayerId)` — server-internal direct method, fire-and-forget, no ack. The emission is recorded **before** the reward is granted; it is wrapped in `pcall` so an ED fault is **non-blocking** (the gather still grants) and logged (rate-limited, ≤1/10 s/player). `Emit` MUST be non-yielding for the emit-before-grant ordering to be deterministic. RN exposes **no** client-callable emit path; the client signals intent (`RequestGather`), the server emits as a consequence.
- **Crafting & Items (Crafting C.13, locked).** After the emission, the server fires `OnGatherCompleted(playerId, nodeId, materialType, count)`; Crafting adds `count` to `PersonalInventory[playerId]`, capped at `MATERIAL_STACK_MAX = 20` (overflow silently discarded by Crafting). RN owns yield/respawn/placement; Crafting owns the 3 material definitions and the inventory write. RN MUST NOT re-raise the emission on overflow. **Bidirectional:** RN lists Crafting as a hard dependency (Crafting → RN: material defs; RN → Crafting: gather completions).
- **Player Controller (PC C.7 / DC-5, locked).** PC owns the input gesture (`TAP_HOLD_COMMIT_DURATION = 500 ms`, `GATHER_PROXIMITY_RADIUS = 4 studs`) and the `GatherNodeArmed` / `GatherNodeDisarmed` reveal/arm signals. RN owns server validation and completion. Dead/respawning players (PC S4) are denied `GatherNodeArmed` and rejected server-side as defense-in-depth. The `firstArmTimestamp` hold anchor is cleared on successful gather (T2), post-commit cancel (T3), disconnect, and death (T4) / safety valve (T5) — so a respawn cannot inherit a stale arm window; a **pre-commit `GatherNodeDisarmed`** clears only `positionAtArm` + the prompt, NOT the hold anchor (round-5 B3; see CR.4 step 1 / firstArm anchor block).
- **Resource Management (soft / indirect).** No direct call. RN's gather→craft→use timing composes into RM's `canisterCycleTime` (Formulas §), which RN must keep above RM's HARD floor (`> 9.09 s`). RN's authored extraction values resolve RM's stale dependency-table provisional ("~8 s", RM line 241) — RN flags this for RM's next revision but does not edit RM (it is APPROVED).
- **Predator AI (indirect).** No direct interface. The predator consumes the ED field RN feeds; louder tiers ripple wider and pull it harder. RN's only obligation is feeding ED the correct per-tier magnitude at the node's world position.

## Formulas

### D.1 — `canisterCycleTime` (the per-cycle floor formula; co-primary gate H.12)

The best-case (clustered nodes, zero travel) gather→craft→use cycle for one Oxygen Canister:

`canisterCycleTime_best = BIOMASS_PER_CANISTER × (TAP_HOLD_COMMIT_DURATION + NODE_EXTRACT_TIME_LIGHT) + CRAFT_TIME_CANISTER`

**Variables:**

| Variable | Symbol | Type | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `BIOMASS_PER_CANISTER` | n_b | int | 2 (Crafting-locked) | BIOMASS units per Oxygen Canister |
| `TAP_HOLD_COMMIT_DURATION` | h | float | 0.3–0.8 s (PC-locked = 0.5) | Tap-hold commit |
| `NODE_EXTRACT_TIME_LIGHT` | x_L | float | 3.25–8.0 s (TAP_HOLD-coupled; worst-case at TAP_HOLD=0.3; default = 3.5) | Server-side extraction, Light tier |
| `CRAFT_TIME_CANISTER` | c | float | 2 s (Crafting-locked) | Canister craft dwell |
| `canisterCycleTime` | — | float | **must be > 9.09 s** | Full best-case cycle |

**Worked example (single serial producer):** `2 × (0.5 + 3.5) + 2 = 10.0 s`, producing 10 oxygen → 1.0 oxygen/s — net-negative against the 2.2/s BC3 drain. Margin on the *per-cycle* number = +0.91 s above 9.09 s (thin; OQ.2).

**D.1a — Why the per-cycle number is NOT the real floor (RN-1, parallel production). `CRAFT_CONCURRENCY_CAP` is squad-size-dependent — pin it to the full-squad worst case (round-4 ruling).** `canisterCycleTime` above models **one** player gathering 2 BIOMASS serially. The system permits **concurrent producers**, but the concurrency cap is **not a constant 2** — it is RM's `CRAFT_CONCURRENCY_CAP = min(2, ceil(squadSize / 2))` (RM DEG-1): a **2-player** squad gets **cap = 1** (one craft slot), and only a **3–4-player** squad saturates to **cap = 2**. So the 2-slot burst is a 3–4-player regime, not a 2-player one. In that full-squad regime, with the BIOMASS for two Canisters gathered in parallel (≈ 4.0 s) and fed into the 2 craft slots, a squad can momentarily produce 2 Canisters in ≈ `4.0 + 2 = 6.0 s` → a transient BURST rate of `2 × 10 / 6.0 ≈ 3.33 oxygen/s`. Note: **3.33 u/s is a momentary transient** (2 canisters in a 6 s burst, full squad only), NOT a sustained rate — SUSTAINED production is craft-cap-bound at `≤ CRAFT_CONCURRENCY_CAP × 10 / canisterCycleTime ≈ 1.67 u/s` at the full-squad cap=2 (RM DEG-1 / D.6 — RN must NOT re-derive this independently). Bursts are INTENDED (RM allows full-squad BC1/BC2 net-positive under loud play — this is not a bug RN's node cap must prevent). **Why the floor assert (H.12) still pins cap = 2:** squad size is unknown at `KnitInit`, and the net-negativity floor is hardest to hold at the *highest* production rate, which is the saturated `cap = 2` (3–4-player) case. Asserting the floor at the worst-case constant `cap = 2` is therefore correct and conservative — a config that keeps BC3 net-negative at cap=2 is automatically net-negative at the lower cap=1. A naïve per-cycle assert that also conflated squad size with cap would be wrong in both directions; this one is pinned deliberately. So the per-cycle number is **necessary but not the floor-bearing invariant**.

**D.1b — The SUSTAINED faucet and the BC3 supply bound (RN-1; co-primary floor obligation).** A net-positive *burst* is intended (a squad stockpiling Canisters before a push is good play; RM D.6 allows full-squad BC1/BC2 net-positive under loud play — do NOT treat this as a bug RN's node cap must prevent). What the run architecture requires is captured in the **RM D.6 restated invariant** (authoritative, attributed to RM D.6):

> *"The run always trends toward a forced, loud finale. A full squad may sustain or slightly grow the pool at BC1/BC2 ONLY by being maximally loud (Pillar 1: maximally hunted); a 2-player squad is net-negative at every band; once escalation reaches BC4 no throughput stops the clock; quiet never sustains the pool at any band."*

**RN's OWNED floor obligation is keeping `canisterCycleTime > 9.09 s`** so that full-squad BC3 remains net-negative (H.12, the per-cycle gate — this is RN's primary owned contribution). RM's `CRAFT_CONCURRENCY_CAP` (= 2 at a 3–4-player full squad; = 1 at 2 players, per `min(2, ceil(squadSize/2))`) is the **production backbone** (sustained restore ≤ `CRAFT_CONCURRENCY_CAP × 10 / canisterCycleTime ≈ 1.67 u/s` at the full-squad cap=2, regardless of node count — RM DEG-1 / D.6); RN does not re-derive or override this. The 9.09 s constant is the cap=2 worst case: `CRAFT_CONCURRENCY_CAP × CANISTER_RESTORE_OXYGEN / OXYGEN_DRAIN_BC3 = 2 × 10 / 2.2 = 9.09 s`. BC3 net-negativity is CONDITIONAL on `canisterCycleTime > 9.09 s` (RM rounds to ~9.1 s); RN's worked value 10.0 s clears it.

**BIOMASS respawn throughput (the supply-side guard — H.30):** the `lightNodeCount` is NOT the production ceiling (the craft cap is, RM-owned). The node-count constraint is a SUPPLY-SIDE guard ensuring BIOMASS respawn can keep the 2 craft slots fed without the faucet misbehaving, AND it enforces the BC3-band supply bound consistent with RM (full-squad BC3 net-negative):

`sustainedBiomass = lightNodeCount × NODE_YIELD_LIGHT / NODE_RESPAWN_TIME_LIGHT` (units/s, steady state)
`sustainedOxygen   = sustainedBiomass × CANISTER_RESTORE_OXYGEN / BIOMASS_PER_CANISTER`

`lightNodeCount × NODE_YIELD_LIGHT / NODE_RESPAWN_TIME_LIGHT × (CANISTER_RESTORE_OXYGEN / BIOMASS_PER_CANISTER) < OXYGEN_DRAIN_BC3`
⟺ `lightNodeCount < OXYGEN_DRAIN_BC3 × NODE_RESPAWN_TIME_LIGHT × BIOMASS_PER_CANISTER / (NODE_YIELD_LIGHT × CANISTER_RESTORE_OXYGEN)`
⟺ (at defaults) `lightNodeCount < 2.2 × 30 × 2 / (1 × 10) = 13.2`

→ **at most 13 Light nodes** bounds the BC3-band supply **at the default `OXYGEN_DRAIN_BC3 = 2.2`**. This "13" is a **derived value, not a hardcoded invariant**: `OXYGEN_DRAIN_BC3` is RM-owned and tunable (its stated 1.8–2.8 band moves the cap to ≈ 10.8–16.8), and `NODE_RESPAWN_TIME_LIGHT` / `NODE_YIELD_LIGHT` move it too — so the cap **floats with the live constants**. H.30 therefore asserts the inequality directly from the named constants at server-init (never against a literal 13), and every "13" in this doc is tagged "at defaults." Full-squad BC1/BC2 net-positive under loud play is INTENDED and is NOT prevented by this gate. **Status (one authoritative framing — see H.30):** H.12 and H.30 are **co-primary hard init gates** (both are BLOCKING; neither alone is sufficient — H.12 bounds the per-cycle rate, H.30 bounds the supply that feeds it). That formal co-primacy is *not* in tension with H.30 being, in normal tuning, the **later-binding** of the two: the RM craft cap saturates the faucet at ~10 effective Light nodes, below the 13-at-defaults supply cap, so H.12/craft-cap typically binds first and H.30 functions as the supply-side backstop. Both labels describe the same fact from different angles — co-primary by *gate status*, backstop by *which binds first in practice* — and the doc uses them in exactly that sense.

**Interacting knobs (4th entry — systems B5):** `NODE_YIELD_LIGHT ↔ NODE_RESPAWN_TIME_LIGHT ↔ lightNodeCount ↔ BC3 supply bound` — derived cap = `OXYGEN_DRAIN_BC3 × BIOMASS_PER_CANISTER × NODE_RESPAWN_TIME_LIGHT / (NODE_YIELD_LIGHT × CANISTER_RESTORE_OXYGEN)`. Change any one, re-derive and re-assert H.30.

**Config-validation invariants (HARD — assert at server init via `error()` in `KnitInit`; H.12 + H.30):** a top-level guard kicks joining players with a config-error message until the service initializes successfully (Roblox cannot abort place load). Server-init asserts ALL THREE, computed from named constants (never hardcoded literals; explicitly reading `NODE_YIELD_LIGHT` and `TAP_HOLD_COMMIT_DURATION` so a yield- or hold-change cannot slip past):
0. **Domain guard (preconditions for the other two — H.12/H.30):** `NODE_YIELD_LIGHT ≥ 1`, `NODE_YIELD_MEDIUM ≥ 1`, `NODE_YIELD_HEAVY ≥ 1`, and `NODE_RESPAWN_TIME_LIGHT > 0`, `NODE_RESPAWN_TIME_MEDIUM > 0`, `NODE_RESPAWN_TIME_HEAVY > 0` (all three respawns guarded, not just Light — a zero respawn also divides by zero in the D.2/D.3 throughput formulas). A `NODE_YIELD_LIGHT = 0` would divide by zero in the supply-bound derivation **and** make the H.30 inequality pass *vacuously* (`0 < OXYGEN_DRAIN_BC3` for any node count) — so the supply gate is meaningless without this guard. Asserted first.
1. **Per-cycle gate (RN's primary owned obligation — H.12):** `BIOMASS_PER_CANISTER × (TAP_HOLD_COMMIT_DURATION + NODE_EXTRACT_TIME_LIGHT) + CRAFT_TIME_CANISTER > CRAFT_CONCURRENCY_CAP × CANISTER_RESTORE_OXYGEN / OXYGEN_DRAIN_BC3` — evaluated at the **configured** values (with `CRAFT_CONCURRENCY_CAP` pinned to its full-squad max of 2, the worst case — see D.1a), so any `TAP_HOLD`/extract combination that dips toward the safe-range-minimum (`2×(0.3+3.25)+2 = 9.1 s`) is caught at init, not shipped.
2. **BC3 supply bound (sustained-faucet — H.30):** `lightNodeCount × NODE_YIELD_LIGHT / NODE_RESPAWN_TIME_LIGHT × CANISTER_RESTORE_OXYGEN / BIOMASS_PER_CANISTER < OXYGEN_DRAIN_BC3` (well-defined only given invariant 0).

These are the only guard against a tuner silently breaking the economy; they cannot be left to manual re-check.

### D.2 — Time-to-Beacon-materials (parallel-gather floor)

Time for `P` players to gather `n` units of a tier under perfect parallelism (one-gatherer-per-node), zero travel:

`gatherTime(tier, n, P) = ceil(n / min(P, nodeCount_tier)) × gatherDuration(tier)`

where `gatherDuration(tier) = TAP_HOLD_COMMIT_DURATION + NODE_EXTRACT_TIME_tier` → **Light 4.0 s · Medium 5.5 s · Heavy 8.5 s**.

**Beacon bill (2 BIOMASS + 3 MINERAL + 3 RESONANT) raw gather floor:**

| Squad | BIOMASS | MINERAL | RESONANT | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2-player | `1×4.0` | `2×5.5` | `2×8.5` | **32.0 s** |
| 4-player | `1×4.0` | `1×5.5` | `1×8.5` | **18.0 s** |

These are floors (zero travel, nodes always available). **Precondition:** defined only when `min(P, nodeCount_tier) > 0`; the CR.7 minimums (`MIN_LIGHT_NODES = 6`, `MIN_MEDIUM_NODES = 4`, Heavy ≥ 4) guarantee this. Real runs add travel plus the live **BIOMASS-for-Canister vs BIOMASS-for-Beacon allocation tension**. These raw gather floors (32.0 s / 18.0 s) sit well inside any plausible run window; the actual session length depends on the BCT escalation schedule (unauthored — forward-pending on ED/RM BCT accessor), so RN does **not** assert specific minute figures. **RESONANT is the *risk* bottleneck, not a supply bottleneck** (RN-3): with ≥4 Heavy nodes a 4-player squad clears all 3 RESONANT in one parallel 8.5 s burst and a 2-player squad does 2 + 1 across two distinct nodes, so the 120 s Heavy respawn is **never forced under normal play assuming at most 1 Heavy node is in an adverse state at Beacon-push time**; the exactly-4 case leaves a 2-player squad a 1-node margin — **prefer ≥5 Heavy nodes (CR.7)**. Supply does not bind under these conditions. What gates RESONANT is the *exposure cost* of gathering it (loudest emission, longest stationary commit, gathered late when the field is already hot), not waiting on respawn. Accordingly `NODE_RESPAWN_TIME_HEAVY` is a backstop, not a balancing lever (Tuning Knobs / OQ.9).

### D.3 — Per-gatherer material throughput

`throughput(tier) = NODE_YIELD_tier / gatherDuration(tier)` (units/s, zero travel/contention):

| Tier | `gatherDuration` | `throughput` |
|---|---|---|
| Light (BIOMASS) | 4.0 s | 0.250 u/s |
| Medium (MINERAL) | 5.5 s | 0.182 u/s |
| Heavy (RESONANT) | 8.5 s | 0.118 u/s |

With realistic travel (5–20 s/node), effective throughput falls ≈40–60 %. This is the number level designers tune node density against (see Tuning Knobs travel constraint). **Values are at DEFAULT config; re-derive at non-default `TAP_HOLD` / extract settings.**

### D.4 — Gather-emission noise budget (Pillar 3)

`emissionsForBill = Σ (count_material × MAGNITUDE_GATHER_tier)`

Full Beacon bill = `2(0.15) + 3(0.25) + 3(0.40) = 2.25` cumulative t-units of initial magnitude, injected across 8 gathers. A 4-player squad firing the bill in an ≈18 s burst injects this 2.25-unit pre-decay budget into the field. **What that injection then *does* — how far it moves the Calm/Tense band, whether it "races the predator" — is ED + Predator-AI-owned and currently unauthored; RN does not claim that consequence here (round-4 honesty; the predator-race read is Beat 2 / H.29b / OQ.1, not a delivered RN interaction).** Decay (`DISTURBANCE_HALF_LIFE_DEFAULT`) and spatial merge (`INFLUENCE_RADIUS = 24`) are ED-owned; **this formula is the pre-decay injection budget only** — RN's owned, testable contribution is the per-tier magnitude it feeds ED (H.4 / H.29a), nothing downstream of the emit.

## Edge Cases

- **E.1 — Node camping (single Heavy node).** If a player waits at a depleted Heavy node for its 120 s respawn: not prevented. The squad's shared oxygen pool drains the whole time (RM), so camping is passively self-punishing — an accepted, economically-costly choice, not an exploit. (A camped player with lantern raised still emits Light pulses — PC/ED-owned, not RN's concern.)
- **E.2 — Two players race the same node.** If two `RequestGather({nodeId})` for one node arrive the same server tick: Luau sequences them; the first passes CR.5 and locks (T1), the second sees `Gathering` and is rejected `node-reserved`. Exactly one lock, one emission, one grant. The rejected player navigates elsewhere.
- **E.3 — Gather completes exactly as the node depletes.** Vacuous under single-charge: depletion *is* the completion (T2 is the only depletion path). No conflict rule needed.
- **E.4 — Inventory full at commit vs mid-extract.** If the gatherer is at `MATERIAL_STACK_MAX = 20` for the tier's material at `RequestGather` time: rejected `inventory-full` pre-lock — no state change, no timer, no emit. If inventory fills *during* extraction (defensive only — no trade mechanic in MVP): at T2 the emission still fires and the node still depletes (per the locked emit-before-grant contract); Crafting silently discards the overflow unit. RN does **not** suppress the depletion or re-raise the emission.
- **E.5 — Gather during the Beacon survival window (BC4).** Not blocked — gathering Canisters during the loud finale is intended. The `Gather` emission fires normally; the in-window Canister-*use* burst (`MAGNITUDE_CANISTER_INWINDOW_USE`) is Crafting/RM-owned, not RN's. If the run *ends* (victory or defeat) during an in-progress extraction, run-end fires T4 for every node in `Gathering`: no yield, no emit for the interrupted gather. **Same-tick race (States § same-tick rule):** if run-end and T2 (extraction complete) fall on the same tick, the outcome is **order-dependent but always exactly one of {grant, cancel}**: if run-end's `runActive = false` flip ran first, T2's step-(0) guard aborts (cancel — no grant, no emit, no `OnGatherCompleted`); if T2's resolve ran first, the grant completes legitimately before `runActive` flips. ("Run-end wins" describes only the abort case, not an unconditional priority over a legitimately-completing gather.)
- **E.6 — Gatherer disconnects mid-extract.** `Players.PlayerRemoving` fires T4: node force-releases to `Available` immediately, no yield, no emit, lock + arm record cleared. (Explicit handling beats waiting for the T5 safety valve — a disconnect should not lock the node for others.)
- **E.7 — Gatherer dies mid-extract.** The PC death signal (or `Players.PlayerRemoving`) fires T4: same as E.6. The arm record is cleared so the player's respawn cannot inherit a stale arm window. T5 (safety valve) is the backstop if the primary death signal misfires.
- **E.8 — ED `Emit` fails at T2.** The emit is `pcall`-wrapped: on failure the gather **still grants** (non-blocking, per ED C.3.1), and the failure is logged (rate-limited ≤1/10 s/player). Not client-exploitable — the client has no path to force a server-internal `Emit` to fail; only a server-side ED fault triggers this, and silently granting on that fault is the correct degrade.
- **E.9 — Forged/teleported position or spammed `nodeId`.** Payload carries `{nodeId}` only; the server reads `HumanoidRootPart.Position` and re-derives proximity. An invented `nodeId` fails the registry check; a node out of server-measured range fails the proximity check. **Honesty caveat (RN-5):** under Roblox's *default* network ownership the client owns its own character's physics, so `HumanoidRootPart.Position` is **client-reported and can be nudged by a position exploit** — the proximity check is a strong filter (a client cannot invent a node or gather one it is genuinely nowhere near without also visibly teleporting its avatar) but it is **not** an absolute anti-teleport guarantee. Realistic ceiling: spoofing is bounded by Roblox's built-in character-physics sanity and by the fact that a spoofed position moves the visible avatar (observable, reportable). Hardening levers if exploited at playtest: set server network ownership of the character (`:SetNetworkOwner(nil)`) during gather, or add a server-side velocity/teleport-delta sanity check (OQ.10). No client path can forge the `nodeId` registry or `Available`-state checks.
- **E.10 — Instant-fire (gather without holding).** If a client fires `RequestGather` immediately after `GatherNodeArmed`: rejected by the server arm-timestamp gate (`now − armTimestamp < TAP_HOLD_COMMIT_DURATION`). The arm event is server-generated and the timestamp server-owned, so the hold cannot be forged.
- **E.11 — Range-exit during extraction.** If the gatherer leaves `GATHER_PROXIMITY_RADIUS + GATHER_JITTER_TOLERANCE` while extracting: the server proximity watcher fires T3 — no yield, no emit, lock cleared. This is also the implicit "abort" gesture (moving away cancels), so no cancel RemoteEvent exists.
- **E.12 — Lantern lowered after arm (dark-zone node).** If a player arms a dark-zone node with the lantern raised, then lowers it before firing: the server re-validates lantern state at `RequestGather` time (CR.5) and rejects. Arming does not bank the right to gather dark.
- **E.13 — Node streamed out on a client but gathered server-side.** Server truth is authoritative; the node Instance always exists server-side regardless of client streaming. A client that believes a node is still `Available` (last state before stream-out) and fires `RequestGather` for a since-depleted node is rejected by the `Available`-state check (CR.5). Clients buffer the last `OnNodeStateChanged` per `nodeId` and reconcile on stream-in.
- **E.14 — Run reset while a node is mid-respawn.** The respawn `task.delay` carries a per-node generation token; `ResetAllNodes()` increments every token, so in-flight respawn callbacks no-op when they fire into the new run. No zombie respawns, no manual timer cancellation needed.
- **E.15 — Safety-valve force-release.** If a lock somehow outlives `NODE_EXTRACT_TIME_tier + GATHER_LOCK_TIMEOUT_BUFFER` (a bug in the completion/cancel path): T5 force-releases the node to `Available` (no yield, no emit) and logs a warning, bounding worst-case lockout.

## Visual/Audio Requirements

### Visual — tier identity (shape-primary, hue-secondary, colorblind-safe)

Each tier is identified at a distance by **silhouette first**, bioluminescent hue second (accessibility floor: tier identity must not rely on hue alone):
- **Light (Biomass)** — low rounded **bulb cluster** (organic), soft even green glow (~150°), tight ~3-stud emission. Quietest.
- **Medium (Mineral)** — angular **faceted shard cluster**, refracted blue-violet (~220°), ~4-stud emission.
- **Heavy (Resonant Ore)** — tall **singular fractured column** (monolith), vivid violet-magenta (~285°), brightest, ~6-stud emission that extends past the player's own lantern pool — **the node advertises before the player even gathers** (the Pillar-3 "loud before you touch it" read). All three hues clear the predator-amber and HUD-violet exclusion zones.

### Visual — state language (glanceable, motion-reduction fallback for each)

- **Available** — resting emission, slow ~4 s pulse (Calm cadence).
- **Gathering** — a white **fill ring** at the base fills over the extraction timer (the time-remaining read), and the node's pulse **accelerates by tier** (Light ~1.5 s / Medium ~1.2 s / Heavy ~0.8 s — the felt-exposure read). **Motion-reduction fallback (REVISED — a frozen 50 % ring communicates nothing and is indistinguishable from Respawning):** a **segmented ring** that reveals discrete filled segments (e.g. 8 segments → "5 of 8 filled ≈ 60 %"), OR a 1 Hz numeric seconds-remaining readout — readable progress without animation.
- **Depleted** — geometry **collapses in place** (tier shape still recognizable), emission near-zero (dead-flora grey). Inherently static.
- **Respawning** — ~1 s reform (geometry rises, emission brightens). Motion-reduction fallback: a **distinct** held state (e.g. dim-amber half-geometry) visually different from the Gathering segmented ring, so the two are never confused.

### Visual — gather-act feedback & the Pillar-3 ownership boundary

Arm ring (ground-level dashed halo on `GatherNodeArmed`) → +50 % emission brighten during the 500 ms commit → the extraction fill ring → a single 0.1 s completion spike (WCAG-safe single event). **Ownership boundary (load-bearing):** RN owns only *node-local* feedback (fill ring, node pulse, emission). The *world-scale* "the planet heard it" tell — surrounding flora saturation, ambient response — is **ED-owned**. RN must not replicate ED's vocabulary at the node, or the two signals fight. This is the concrete resolution of the Player-Fantasy downstream obligation: the node says "extraction happening," the world (ED) says "the world noticed."

### Visual — mobile budget (iPhone SE-class, 30fps)

Geometry-scale + light-Brightness Tweens only; **no** per-node particle emitters except the completion beat (≤8 particles); **no** `SelectionBox`; **no** Billboard GUI for state (geometry-driven). A node in any state must cost less than one max-saturation flora node. **Dynamic-light budget — scoped to what RN owns (perf):**
- **RN's owned constraint (enforceable):** RN contributes at most **`MAX_NODE_POINTLIGHTS_IN_FRUSTUM = 4`** dynamic PointLights from Resource Nodes within the camera frustum at once; beyond that, RN's range-based `Brightness` LOD zeros the farthest node lights (nearest-N kept). **Preferred mobile path:** replace the per-node PointLight with an **emissive `SurfaceAppearance`** (zero dynamic-light cost), triggered when the platform is mobile (`UserInputService.TouchEnabled` / low-tier device flag) OR whenever the frustum node-light count would exceed the cap. This is the only part of the light budget RN can own and assert.
- **Aggregate cross-system cap (NOT RN-enforceable — explicit obligation hand-off):** the total simultaneous dynamic lights on an iPhone-SE-class device (player lanterns + flora + nodes + any other source) is a **tech-art / performance-analyst budget**, not RN's — RN cannot see or gate lanterns and flora. The SE-class derivation of that aggregate number (how many simultaneous PointLights the lowest-tier target sustains at 30 fps) is **forward-pending the tech-art lighting-budget pass**; RN's `MAX_NODE_POINTLIGHTS_IN_FRUSTUM` must be re-fit under whatever total that pass sets. Owner: technical-artist + performance-analyst (logged OQ.12).

### Audio — per-tier loudness you can hear (Pillar 1 + 3)

A listening squadmate must identify a teammate's gather tier by ear and feel its exposure. **The AUTHORITATIVE spec is the per-tier source level — Light −18 / Medium −13 / Heavy −8 dBFS (~5 dB steps).** The carry distances (~15 / ~25 / ~35–40 studs in Calm) are **DERIVED targets, NOT an independent specification**: they are the audible reach the asset pass must *achieve by tuning* `RolloffStyle` + `RollOffMaxDistance` per emitter, given those source levels — they are not a second constraint that can conflict with the dBFS ladder. (The two read as inconsistent only if both are treated as independent: a 3× distance step under default inverse rolloff implies ≈−10 dB of attenuation, not −5 dB, so the rolloff model — not a second number — is what reconciles source level to reach.) **Asset-pass obligation (audio):** pick the `RolloffStyle`/`RollOffMaxDistance` per tier so the −18/−13/−8 dBFS sources land at roughly the stated reaches AND the per-tier step stays above the practical JND at the listener — document the chosen rolloff model in the asset spec before sign-off. Material fiction: Light = soft organic tear; Medium = brittle crystalline scrape with chip transients; Heavy = sustained resonant tone under a grind — **a low-MID resonance whose fundamental sits ABOVE the provisional 500 Hz protected floor (not a sub-bass "hum"; "resonant" here = a pitched low-mid body, not low-frequency energy)** — see the hard constraint below. The "advertise before you touch it" read is carried by a **mid-band 3D idle shimmer** on the Heavy node (routed to `GatherSFX`, above the protected floor, cut once extraction begins). **Shimmer constraints (audio F2; (b) revised round-4):** (a) **proximity-gated** — audible only within ~10–12 studs (the player's awareness zone), NOT the full node carry distance, so ≥4 Heavy nodes do not cumulate into a mid-band masking layer; (b) **ducks 8–12 dB during predator Hunt (does NOT silence entirely).** Round-4 reconciliation: silencing the shimmer in Hunt would kill the Beat-1 "advertise before you touch it" affordance *exactly when it is needed most* — the Beacon-push happens under Hunt, and the squad must still locate Heavy nodes to gather RESONANT. Ducking keeps it a faint approach affordance while ensuring it never competes with the predator's own audio (which sits above it in the mix and is never masked); the shimmer remains a gather affordance, never a survival cue. **Verification (advisory / Feel — asset pass):** that the ducked shimmer stays audible enough to locate a Heavy node in Hunt yet quiet enough not to mask the predator is an audio-director sign-off item against real predator SFX (the duck-target bus is forward-pending Predator AI, OQ.11) — captured as a Visual/Feel evidence gate, not a BLOCKING AC. (c) a **simultaneous-shimmer voice cap of ≤2 nearest** Heavy nodes.

### Audio — two-phase arc, priority, frequency protection

Commit cue (150–250 ms) → seamless **extraction loop** (3.5/5/8 s, slow 1–2 dB swell on Medium/Heavy = "building consequence") → **completion stinger** (fires on server-authoritative `OnGatherCompleted`, never client hold-release) → interrupt stinger (on T3/T4 cancel). The loop sits in the **mid-priority `GatherSFX` group** and ducks 4–6 dB under predator audio in Hunt (it stays a coordination signal, never masks the predator). **Hard constraint (PROVISIONAL — RN-2 round-2, audio F1):** extraction loops carry no prominent tonal content below a **provisional 500 Hz protected floor**, raised from the round-1 250 Hz (250 Hz is low-mid, not "sub-bass," and a 250 Hz floor leaves the breathing/footstep low-mid body unprotected). The **authoritative protected band is forward-pending on the unauthored Predator AI audio spec (OQ.11)** — 500 Hz is an interim conservative placeholder and MUST be verified against actual predator SFX before final asset sign-off. All gather SFX are 3D positional (squad triangulation affordance); the predator consumes the ED field, never gather audio.

### Audio — accessibility & budget

Captions-mode visual equivalents for every cue (commit tag, the extraction bar doubles as the loop proxy — **REQUIRED in captions/reduced-motion mode** per UI Requirements, completion/interrupt single-flash <3 Hz). **≥ 12 clips at MVP (a FLOOR, not a final count — round-5)**: the enumerated baseline is 3 per tier × commit/loop/complete (9) + Heavy idle shimmer (1) + shared interrupt (1) + release (1) = 12, **all routed to `GatherSFX`**. **Forward obligation (audio asset-pass, owner audio-director):** the true count is higher and is owed at the asset spec — at minimum the "shared interrupt" should become a **tier-keyed interrupt** (×3, to preserve the tier-identity the swell builds), and **arm-confirm, respawn/reform, and the five reject cues** are currently unaccounted. Not RN's: ambient-stem crossfade, predator cues, HUD/UI sounds. (The undercount this REC previously flagged is now folded into the "≥ 12 clips" forward obligation above.)

### Level-design flag (cross-department)

Heavy nodes MUST NOT share respawn timers within `HEAVY_NODE_MIN_RESPAWN_STAGGER = 15 s` — **enforced as a server-init config gate (H.22-class), not a prose advisory** — because simultaneous multi-node completion spikes during a parallel Beacon-push (3–4 near-simultaneous Heavy completions) accumulate toward the WCAG 2.3.1 ≤3-flashes/s threshold. **Per-node "WCAG-safe single flash" ≠ aggregate-safe (BINDING):** the HUD/PC layer **MUST** keep the *aggregate* completion-flash rate across simultaneous completions within WCAG 2.3.1 (≤ 3 flashes/s in any region) — it MUST do so by gating/merging concurrent completion flashes OR by making the completion indicator non-flash (chime + colour-hold). This is a hard requirement, not an option; RN supplies the per-completion event and owns only the per-node single-flash. The aggregate-budget AC is **forward-pending the HUD GDD** (H.36-class; logged OQ.6) since RN does not own the HUD render layer.

## UI Requirements

RN owns no screens. It contributes data and state events that the **HUD** and the **PC input layer** render; this section specifies what RN must supply and the parity/accessibility constraints it inherits.

- **Gather prompt (arm affordance) + commit-and-wait model.** On `GatherNodeArmed(nodeId)`, a contextual non-modal prompt appears ("Hold E / tap-hold to gather"), mirroring the bench-armed prompt pattern. RN supplies the arm/disarm signals; the **PC input layer / UX** owns the prompt widget. **Commit-and-wait (F-D, decided):** the 0.5 s tap-hold COMMITS; extraction then runs **hands-free** — the player need NOT maintain finger/button contact for the 3.5/5/8 s extraction (an 8.5 s sustained hold on iPhone-SE-class would be an accessibility failure). The player must remain in range; leaving cancels (T3).
  - **Hands-free in-range tether indicator (ux F5 — fills the invisible-tether gap).** Because extraction is hands-free, the only thing the player must actively do for up to 8.5 s is *stay in range* — but with no held input there is no longer any on-screen signal that a gather is even in progress or that range matters. So the **arm ring PERSISTS through the entire extraction** (it does not vanish at commit) and reads as the live "you are gathering, stay here" tether; the world-space fill ring (Visual §) sits inside it. This is RN-supplied state (the node is `Gathering` with this player as gatherer); PC/UX renders the persistent ring. **Commit-confirm obligation (round-5, named forward obligation on PC/UX):** the ring MUST change to a visually distinct *extraction* state at the moment of commit (e.g. dashed/advisory → solid/active, or a brightness step) so the player can confirm the 0.5 s commit actually landed before the hands-free extraction begins — distinguishable without colour alone. Without this, an ambiguous mobile tap-hold that didn't register reads as "I gathered, then got nothing" when the player walks away (a false T3). RN supplies the `Gathering`-with-this-gatherer state transition that triggers it.
  - **T3 pre-cancel proximity warning (ux F5).** Approaching the cancel boundary (`GATHER_PROXIMITY_RADIUS + GATHER_JITTER_TOLERANCE`) MUST give a pre-warning *before* T3 fires — e.g. the tether ring pulses / shifts to a warning colour-plus-shape in the outer ~1 stud — so range-exit cancel is never silent (a silent cancel on mobile reads as a bug). Distinguishable without colour alone; WCAG-safe.
  - **Cross-input parity (hard rule).** No hover-only affordance; must work on **keyboard** (E-hold, or the remappable equivalent via `ContextActionService`), touch tap-hold, mouse hold, and **gamepad** (the commit is a button hold-to-`TAP_HOLD_COMMIT_DURATION`, e.g. ButtonA; after commit the player walks — stick or WASD — hands free; identical hands-free model across **all four** input classes). PC owns the exact bindings (round-5: verify at PC revision that the gather-commit binding does not collide with other contextual actions available in the same spatial context — e.g. a bench-armed prompt near a node). **Reject cues must also be legible without audio** (keyboard-only / muted players): every typed reject reason resolves to a visual cue, never audio-only.
- **Dark-zone precondition indicator (ux F3).** In dark zones the arm prompt is suppressed when the lantern is down (CR.4, PC DC-5) — but a glowing node *looks* interactive, so suppression-with-no-signal is a legibility void. When a player is in range of a dark-zone node with lantern down, RN/PC MUST render a low-prominence **"raise lantern" precondition indicator** (visually distinct from both Depleted and out-of-range) rather than nothing, so the player can discover the requirement without trial-and-error failure.
- **Extraction progress indicator.** The 3.5 / 5 / 8 s extraction needs a progress read. The world-space fill ring (Visual §) is RN-rendered; the HUD progress bar is **HUD-owned**, **optional in normal mode but REQUIRED in captions/reduced-motion mode** (it is the captions-mode proxy for the audio extraction loop, so it cannot be optional there). RN supplies, in the `OnNodeStateChanged` Gathering payload, `extractionDuration` and `extractionStartTimestamp` (= `workspace:GetServerTimeNow()` at lock) so HUD computes progress as `(GetServerTimeNow() − start) / duration` **without a drift-prone local timer**.
- **Reject feedback (ux F1 — DISTINGUISHABLE cues, not one generic flash).** A rejected `RequestGather` returns a typed reason (`node-reserved | inventory-full | rate-limited | out-of-range | lantern-required`). RN supplies the reason code; **HUD/UX** MUST map each to a *distinguishable, actionable* non-modal cue (colour must not be the sole differentiator): `node-reserved` → lock-icon / "[name] gathering" overlay; `out-of-range` → directional/proximity nudge (not a fail flash); `lantern-required` → "raise lantern" icon (same vocabulary as the precondition indicator); `inventory-full` → fail flash + inventory-full icon; `rate-limited` → **silent on the server-reject path, but the client input layer MUST locally debounce so a legit rapid re-tap never sends a second `RequestGather` in the first place.** Rationale (ux F1): the limit (1 / 1.5 s) sits far above any legitimate gather cadence — extraction alone is 3.5–8 s — so a server-side `rate-limited` reject means a malfunctioning or hostile client, not a human input the player needs feedback on; surfacing it as a "failed" cue would mislabel legit retries as errors. The fix for the "silence reads as input failure" risk is client debounce (no spurious second send), not a reject cue. Never a modal prompt on the primary gather action (disqualified at concept tier). All cues single-flash, WCAG-safe.
- **Material gain.** A successful gather increments the gatherer's `PersonalInventory`; the player's own 3 material counts are shown in the **HUD/Crafting** inventory surface. RN's only contribution is firing `OnGatherCompleted`; it renders no inventory UI.

All RN-contributed UI must scale via the project's `UIScale` / aspect-ratio constraints, meet the 44×44 pt minimum touch target (the gather prompt), and remain legible at iPhone SE-class distance.
