## Overview

Terranova on Roblox is built around a single mechanical premise: the player squad's
survival actions disturb the alien ecology, and the ecology's apex predator hunts
along that disturbance. The Roblox-scoped MVP collapses the original 21-system
Unity vision down to **7 core systems** by (a) deferring resource breadth (oxygen
only), (b) cutting base-building, (c) consolidating crafting/inventory/items into
one GDD, and (d) leaning on Roblox built-ins for input, save/load, settings, world
streaming, and respawn. The systems below are designed for co-op-from-day-one with
server-authoritative architecture (Knit Services on the server, Knit Controllers
on the client).

## Dependency Map

> **Convention — Inverted Bus.** "Depends on" in this index = a system reads from
> or queries another system's API. Publishing emissions to the Ecological Disturbance
> bus is **not** treated as a dependency in this column — publishers do not couple
> to the bus's runtime state, only to its emission contract (which is a forward-
> obligation tracked in each system's GDD F.2a). This keeps the bus a pure
> publish-target with zero in-bound deps in the table; consumer-side coupling is
> what gates design order. Forward-obligations (publish-side contracts) are listed
> per-system in the GDD `F.2a` Deferred Contracts subsection, not here.

### Foundation Layer (no dependencies)

1. **Ecological Disturbance** — pure event bus + signal definitions. Other systems publish events to and subscribe from it. Dependency-free by construction.
2. **Player Controller** — wraps Roblox `Humanoid` + custom interaction. Depends on no MVP-internal systems for design. (Publishes `Sprint` + `Light` emissions to ED — forward-obligation only, not a consumer-side dep.)

### Core Layer (depends on Foundation)

3. **Crafting & Items** — depends on Player Controller for interaction. Defines item schema (including oxygen consumable + escape beacon recipe). (Publishes `Beacon` + `Craft` emissions to ED — forward-obligation only.)
4. **Resource Management** — depends on Crafting & Items for the oxygen item definition.

### Feature Layer (depends on Core)

5. **Resource Node** — depends on Crafting & Items for yield definitions. (Publishes `Gather` emissions to ED — forward-obligation only.)
6. **Predator AI** — depends on Player Controller (target tracking) **and** Ecological Disturbance (queries the field for hottest hotspot, subscribes to `TierCrossedEvent` etc.). The first true ED consumer.

### Presentation Layer (depends on Feature)

7. **HUD** — depends on Resource Management (oxygen meter), Ecological Disturbance (subscribes to `OnMeterUpdate` + `OnDisturbanceAlert`), Crafting & Items (inventory display).

### Circular Dependencies

- **None.** ED is a publish-target with zero in-bound deps; only Predator AI and HUD consume from it via queries/subscriptions.
