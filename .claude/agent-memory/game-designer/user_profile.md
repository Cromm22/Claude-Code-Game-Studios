User is designing Terranova, a first-person alien survival game for PC (Steam/Epic), as a solo or small team project. They are working at a mid-to-senior game design level — comfortable with systems design theory and want specific, implementable output (named items, exact gate conditions, concrete formulas) rather than high-level theory. They bring strong design vision (5 clear pillars, a unique hook) and expect the agent to deliver GDD-ready material a programmer could act on.

Their design philosophy: no tutorial text, world teaches through consequence, mastery through demonstrated understanding not grind. Anti-patterns to avoid: hand-waving ("should feel good"), vague conditions ("survived a while"), false choices in gate design.
